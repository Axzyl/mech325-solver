General Equations for Boundary-Lubricated Bearings

1. Thermal Length Constraint

$$L_{min} \geq \frac{720 f_s n_d F N}{J (\bar{h}C_R) (T_f - T_\infty)}$$

2. Bearing Diameter

$$D = d + c_d$$

3. Projected Area

$$A_p = D \times L$$

4. Nominal Bearing Pressure

$$P = \frac{n_d F}{A_p} = \frac{n_d F}{D L}$$

5. Maximum Bearing Pressure

$$P_{max} = \frac{4 n_d F}{\pi D L}$$

6. Surface Velocity

$$V = \frac{\pi d N}{12} \quad \text{[ft/min]}$$

7. PV Factor

$$PV = P \times V \quad \text{[psi·ft/min]}$$

8. Frictional Torque

$$T_f = \frac{f_s n_d F d}{2} \quad \text{[lbf·in]}$$

9. Frictional Power Loss

$$H_f = \frac{2\pi T_f N}{12 \times 33000} = \frac{T_f N}{63025} \quad \text{[hp]}$$

Design Constraints

$$P \leq P_{max,allow}$$ $$P_{max} \leq P_{max,allow}$$ $$PV \leq PV_{max}$$
