General Equations Reference

Torque from Power

$$T = \frac{63000 P}{n}$$

Tangential Force

$$W_t = \frac{2T}{D}$$

Gear Forces

$$W_r = W_t \tan(\phi)$$

Size Factor

$$C_s = \left(\frac{D}{0.3}\right)^{-0.11} \quad \text{for } 0.3 < D \leq 2 \text{ in}$$
D ≤ 0.3": Cs = 1.0 | D > 2": Cs = 0.859 - 0.02125D

Modified Endurance Strength

$$s'_n = s_n \cdot C_m \cdot C_{st} \cdot C_R \cdot C_s$$

Shaft Diameter (with torque/bending)

$$D = \left[\frac{32N}{\pi}\sqrt{\left(\frac{K_t M}{s'_n}\right)^2 + \frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{T}{s_y}\right)^2}\right]^{1/3}$$

Shaft Diameter (shear only)

$$D = \sqrt{\frac{2.94 K_t V N}{s'_n}}$$

Bearing Load Rating

$$C = R \left(\frac{L_D}{L_1}\right)^{1/k}$$

Key Length

$$L_{min} = \frac{4T}{S_y \cdot W \cdot D}$$
