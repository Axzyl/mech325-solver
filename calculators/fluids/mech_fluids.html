General Equations Reference

Basic Length

$$L_{basic} = L_{stroke} \times k$$
k = Stroke Factor based on mounting style (0.50 to 2.00)

Acceleration Factor

$$g = \frac{V^2}{S} \times 0.0000517$$
V = velocity to accelerate to [ft/min]
S = distance over which acceleration occurs [in]

Total Force (with acceleration)

$$F_{total} = F_{max} \times (1 + g)$$

Flow Rate

$$Q_{true} = \frac{V_{ext}}{10} \times Q_{table}$$
Qtable = Flow rate from Table b-5 at 10 ft/min piston speed [GPM]

Fluid Velocity Check

$$v_{actual} = v_{table} \times \frac{V_{ext}}{10} < 15 \text{ ft/s}$$

Minimum Horsepower

$$HP_{min} = \frac{Q \times P}{1714 \times 0.85}$$
Q = Flow rate [GPM]
P = Operating pressure [psi]
0.85 = Assumed pump efficiency
