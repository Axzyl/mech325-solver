General Equations Reference

Tensile Stress Area

$$A_t = \frac{F}{\sigma_d}$$

Shear Area and Nut Length

$$A_s = \frac{F}{\tau_d}$$
$$h = \frac{A_{s,calc}}{A_{s,rated}}$$

Lead Angle

$$\lambda = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{p}{\pi D_p}\right)$$

Torque Equations

$$T_u = \frac{FD_p}{2}\left[\frac{\cos\phi\tan\lambda + f}{\cos\phi - f\tan\lambda}\right] + f_cFR_c$$
$$T_d = \frac{FD_p}{2}\left[\frac{f - \cos\phi\tan\lambda}{\cos\phi + f\tan\lambda}\right] + f_cFR_c$$

Efficiency

$$e = \frac{Fp}{2\pi T_u}$$

Speed Calculations

$$n = \frac{63000P}{T_u}$$
$$V = \frac{np}{60}$$
